Case studies

CogniHab · 2025 – present

CogniHab: VR rehabilitation with real-time body tracking

Context. CogniHab is an in-house rehabilitation product. Patients do guided exercises in VR, so the app has to follow their movement live and work both in the clinic and remotely.

What I did. I drive front-end VR development and lead the front-end engineering team:

  • Built several working prototypes, from concept to execution
  • Real-time scanning of the user's body movement
  • Local and remote connection streaming
  • Video data fetched on demand, so the experience stays smooth
  • Mentor and manage a 3-member development team

Result. The product moved from early ideas to working VR builds, with tracking, streaming and video content all running in one app.

Ediiie · Client: Carespace · 2024 – 2025

Carespace: taking over a project and delivering it alone

Challenge. A healthcare motion-tracking project in Unreal Engine came in half-built from an external team. It needed someone to understand it fast and finish it.

  • Took over the codebase and managed full delivery as the only developer
  • Pose tracking: integrated the MediaPipe plugin
  • Motion: replicated the user's movement on a 3D humanoid rig in real time
  • Networking: built the WebSocket communication layers
  • Support: resolved 400+ technical tickets in a fast, deadline-driven setting

Result. The project was delivered end to end, with live tracking working from camera to rig.

Ediiie · Client: Fractional Uprising Studios · 2023

Open Season: multiplayer for a 5v5 battle royale

Context. A battle royale shooter in Unreal Engine, now on the Epic Games Store, built by a 2-person team under tight deadlines.

  • Engineered the core multiplayer for 5v5 matches
  • Found and fixed critical gameplay desync between players
  • Kept matches stable when network conditions changed

Result. Stable online matches in a shipped game. See it on the Epic Games Store.
